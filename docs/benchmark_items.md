# Benchmark items

Every item of the four suites, with its answer, its tolerance and how many of the 26 model configurations answered it correctly. A tolerance is relative to the answer. The chart items are generated by `benchmark/make_charts.py`, and the tool items by `benchmark/run.py`. The figure and passage items come from open-access papers under CC BY 4.0. Their questions and answers are listed here with the DOI of each paper, and the figure images and passage texts stay outside the repository.
